What we build and maintain

Six areas of work, one engineering standard.

Starlink Installation & Configuration

Site survey, dish mounting, alignment, and full configuration for reliable satellite internet in remote or urban locations.

Network Infrastructure

Structured cabling, routers, switches, and rack builds designed for uptime — planned around how your organization actually works.

CCTV & Security Systems

Camera placement, wiring, and monitoring setup that covers the areas that matter, without unnecessary blind spots or clutter.

Structural mounting for antennas and satellite equipment, built to withstand weather and hold alignment over time.

Maintenance & Support

Scheduled checks and rapid response when something needs attention, so small issues don't become downtime.

Training & Mentorship
